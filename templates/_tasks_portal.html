How are these numbers worked out?
  • Received: tasks assigned to the person in the period.
  • On time: finished by the deadline. Late: finished after it.
  • Overdue: still open (including never answered) and the deadline has passed.
  • On-time % = on time ÷ (completed + overdue). Rejected and cancelled tasks are not counted against anyone.
  • Accepted % = accepted ÷ (accepted + rejected).
