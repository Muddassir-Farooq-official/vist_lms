1 Close the terms

Ends the semester (open enrollments completed) and closes each batch term. Unfinished courses (attendance not locked, results not published) need a reason.

Batch termStatusSubjectsOpen enrollmentsUnfinished

2 Promote students

Everyone moves to the next semester except those you hold back (write why). Failed subjects stay as carry-over for supplementary exams. A batch in its last semester is not promoted. You can undo within 7 days.

3 Start the next session

Creates the next semester's batch terms, opens their subjects from the curriculum (same sections), enrolls the promoted students and keeps the same teachers where they still work here.
