Back up and download

Every night at 2:30 AM the server copies the database and the uploaded files, encrypted, to the backup account (off this server). These buttons ask the server to do something now; it starts within a minute.

A download needs your password again, is ready within a few minutes, can be downloaded only by you, and is deleted from the server after 1 hour. The other administrators are told. Open a .dump in pgAdmin with Restore (into a new, empty database), or with pg_restore. Keep downloaded copies somewhere safe: they hold everyone's data.
