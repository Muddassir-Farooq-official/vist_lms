Charge attendance fines (before final exams)

Each subject below the minimum to pass is fined by its band. Check first: nothing is charged until you press Charge. Running it again never charges twice; if a student has fallen into a worse band since, only the difference is charged. Bands and amounts are in Settings → Attendance.

{{ nav_icon("attendance") }}

Below the lowest band: decisions, and every fine charged

Students below the lowest band are not fined or dropped automatically. Decide for each: allow with a fine (they continue once it is paid), excuse (e.g. medical), or drop from the course. Once a fine is paid or waived, the exam marks decide the result.

Lock Attendance (end of term)

Locking freezes this course's attendance for good. With fines by band (Settings → Attendance), it also charges any fine not charged yet and lists anyone below the lowest band for a decision; with the older rule it fines or drops students below the thresholds. It cannot be undone. Every held class must first be marked (Needs Attention) or cancelled.

Choose a term and course on the Mark attendance tab first; its lock check shows here.

{{ nav_icon("attendance") }}

Cancel a Whole Day (holiday / strike)

Cancels every still-scheduled class on that date. Classes whose attendance was already taken are left alone.

Approved Leave for One Student
