Classes a week & labs

Classes a week start at the course's credit hours; change them where needed. Tick Lab (double) for a lab or practical: each class is two back-to-back periods in a lab room. The home room is used when it is free. A combined class keeps the same settings in all its sections. Changes are saved straight away.

Teacher workload

Credit hours a week each teacher teaches while the chosen term(s) run: every section whose term dates overlap counts, and a combined class counts once. Each teacher should be between 12 and 18 (Settings → Teaching load), or their own range. Giving a teacher a section that takes them above their most is refused unless you give a reason.
