Post depreciation

Once a month, after the month ends. Each asset is charged from where it was left up to the chosen month end (months missed are caught up). Only the latest run can be undone (by the administration), and never inside closed books.

Disposals

Sold, scrapped, donated, lost or written off. The office asks (from the asset's page); the administration approves. On approval the last part of the depreciation is posted, the book value is kept, and money from a sale goes into Accounts as income.

Categories

Each category has a code (used in tag numbers) and a depreciation method. Confirm them with your auditor and change them before posting depreciation.
