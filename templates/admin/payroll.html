The accounts office prepares a month, adjusts anything that needs it (unpaid days, extras) and sends it for approval. The administration approves it (or sends it back with a note), then the accounts office pays the salaries and marks it paid. Everyone gets their payslip when it is paid.

Absences and late arrivals / early leavings come from staff attendance. The rules (casual leaves, how many late marks make a day, security %) are in Settings → Payroll and can be changed any time; press Recalculate on a draft to apply them.

Ask for a salary advance

Money paid to a member of staff before payday. Three steps: the accounts office asks for it here, an administrator approves it, then the accounts office hands it over (it is recorded as an expense) and it is taken back a little at a time from the next payslips. One person may owe at most the limit in Settings → Payroll (normally one month's basic salary), and nobody can ask for or pay an advance to themselves.
