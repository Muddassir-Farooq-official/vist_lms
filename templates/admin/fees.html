Give a scholarship or discount

Students in the same class can pay different amounts: a scholarship or discount takes a percentage or a fixed amount off their unpaid installments, and their challans change to the new amount. It never rewrites what was already paid.

For a tuition discount that continues every semester, choose "Tuition … only (every semester)": exam, library and other fees are left alone.
Empty = from the start of the current term, so this term's installments already due are included. Earlier terms' dues are never touched.
