    How it works

    • You see their screens, with their data, for up to 30 minutes.
    • Nothing can be changed while viewing: saving, marking or submitting is refused.
    • A bar at the top says whose screens you are viewing, with an Exit button.
    • Every view is kept in the activity log: who viewed whom, when, and from where.
    • Administrators' accounts cannot be viewed.
