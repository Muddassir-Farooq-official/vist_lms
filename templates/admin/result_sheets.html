University result (affiliated programs)

Template (.csv)

When the university (KMU, AUST …) announces the result, enter it here so transcripts, GPA/CGPA and promotion use it. One row per student per subject: reg_number (or roll_number), course_code, the university's marks_obtained and marks_total for the subject, and/or its grade; result (PASS / FAIL) is optional (without it, pass/fail follows the grade or the program's pass mark). Every row is checked first, and nothing is saved while any row has a problem. Importing again replaces the earlier university result (when the university revises it).

Grading tables

One table per affiliating body (KMU, AUST, Sarhad, Board …): letters, grade points (fixed or sliding inside a band), divisions, pass marks for theory and practical, the re-sit cap and the semester rules. Every value can be edited at any time. Published results never change. Ready-made tables arrive as Draft: check each row against the official table, untick Draft, then give it to programs.
