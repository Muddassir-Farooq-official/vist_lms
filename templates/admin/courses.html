Open a Semester

Creates one offering for EVERY course in the program's curriculum for that semester. Courses that already have this section are skipped, so it is safe to run twice.

Enroll Students

Enrolls every ACTIVE student matching the filters, even while self-enrollment is closed. Curriculum, credit-hour, seat and timetable-clash rules still apply, and anyone who cannot be enrolled is listed with the reason.

Auto-schedule the whole term

Gives a weekly timetable to every course of the selected term that does not have one yet. Each course is placed at a time when its teacher, its students and a room are all free. Courses that already have a timetable are left alone, so it is safe to run again.

Copy last term’s timetable

Repeats the weekly times and rooms of the same course and section from an earlier term into the selected term. Anything that clashes, or did not run before, is listed so you can place it by hand.
