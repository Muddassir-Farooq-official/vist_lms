      Who approves what

      For each kind of request, choose whether it needs approval and who gives it: as the system does now, anyone with a role (e.g. Principal), one person, the head of the department concerned, or the course's own teacher. A rule can be only for people with one role (e.g. Accounts Officer) and from an amount (e.g. Rs. 5,000 and above). The most specific rule wins. Nobody approves their own request, and an Administrator can always decide so nothing gets stuck. Rules about money wait for a second administrator.
