3. Teachers for every section

Credit hours a week each teacher teaches while the chosen term(s) run: every section whose term dates overlap counts, and a combined class counts once. Each teacher should be between 12 and 18 (Settings → Teaching load), or their own range. Giving a teacher a section that takes them above their most is refused unless you give a reason.

4. Classes a week & labs

Classes a week start from the course catalog: one theory class per theory credit hour plus one lab per lab credit hour (a 3+1 course: 3 theory classes and 1 lab). Change them where needed. Under Labs, say how many of those classes are labs or practicals: a lab is two back-to-back periods in a lab room. For example, 3 classes a week with 1 lab gives two single theory periods and one lab double period; All makes every class a lab. Labs show with a Lab label on everyone's timetable. The home room is used when it is free. A combined class keeps the same settings in all its sections. Changes are saved straight away. The batch terms chosen in step 1 are loaded for you.
