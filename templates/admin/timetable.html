Daily Periods

The institute's daily periods, e.g. Period 1: 8:00 AM–8:50 AM. Every timetable slot picks from these. Add breaks (break, prayer, lunch), a zero period or assembly, and library / self-study periods too, so they show on every timetable. A period can apply to some days only, e.g. a shorter Friday. Change… moves a period (and the classes in it) to new times from a date you choose; you see what will move before anything changes.
