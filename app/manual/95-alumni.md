# Alumni

This chapter is for graduates. When you graduate, your student login becomes an alumni login: sign in as before.

## My alumni page

- **Degree and transcripts**: where your degree and any transcripts are (requested, being prepared, ready to collect, collected or posted), with their serial numbers. You are told when one is ready; bring your CNIC. **My result card (PDF)** downloads your result card; **Ask for a printed transcript** asks the office for printed copies.
- **My details**: your employer, job title, city, further study and contact details. Keep them up to date: the institute uses them to stay in touch. Tick **List me in the alumni directory** to let other graduates find you (name, program, year, work and city); tick the second box as well to show them your email, phone and LinkedIn.
- **Surveys**: answer the institute's alumni survey when one is open (once per survey).
- **Alumni directory**: find classmates who chose to be listed.
