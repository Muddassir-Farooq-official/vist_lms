# Library

**Dashboard** shows loans, returns, requests and the collection at a glance.

- **Issue & Return**: lend a book to a student or staff member and take it back. Fines for late returns are worked out automatically.
- **Borrow Requests**: requests from students and faculty through their own portal.
- **Overdue**: loans past their due date, oldest first.
- **Catalogue**: every book and copy; add, edit and search.
- **Journals & E-Resources**: journal titles and the same live search students and faculty have.
- **Fines**: library fines and their status.

**Announcements**, **Post an announcement**, **Tasks**, **Applications**, **Purchase requests**, **My pay & attendance**, **Calendar**, **My Profile** and **My warnings** are explained in "Tools every staff member has".
