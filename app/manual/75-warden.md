# Hostel warden

**Home** shows rooms, beds, residents and hostel charges at a glance.

- **Hostel**: rooms and beds, who lives where, moving residents in and out, and the history of stays.
  - **Mess**: a resident's monthly mess fee (0 = not in the mess). New residents get the default mess fee from Settings.
  - **Night roll call**: choose the night (today by default), mark each resident present, absent or on leave, and **Save roll call**. Parents and the student are told on their phones when a resident is absent (not on leave), once per night. A roll call can be corrected for three days.
  - **Monthly hostel and mess charges**: choose the month and **Prepare draft**. It lists each resident's room and mess fee (part months by days). The accounts office approves it before anything reaches a student's dues; if they send it back you see why. Preparing again replaces a draft not yet approved.
- **Announcements**: notices for you; **Post an announcement** for residents.

**Tasks**, **Applications**, **Purchase requests**, **My pay & attendance**, **Calendar**, **My Profile** and **My warnings** are explained in "Tools every staff member has".
