# Gate

**Home** shows who is inside right now, today's visitors and staff in / out.

- **Visitors**: write every visitor in the log book when they arrive (name, CNIC, purpose, whom they meet) and mark them out when they leave.
- **Visitor log book**: everyone who came in, with times. Search the last 7 days.
- **Staff in / out**: mark staff in when they arrive and out when they leave. Late arrival and early leaving are worked out from their timing. A wrong mark can be taken back within 10 minutes.

**Announcements**, **Post an announcement**, **Tasks**, **Applications**, **Purchase requests**, **My pay & attendance**, **Calendar**, **My Profile** and **My warnings** are explained in "Tools every staff member has".
