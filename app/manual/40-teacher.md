# Teacher

**Home** shows your classes today, marking still to do and messages.

## Teaching

- **My Courses**: the courses you teach. Add each class's topic, slides, notes and links; students see them straight away. Marking a topic complete feeds Teacher Progress.
- **Attendance**: mark attendance within 15 minutes of a class's start time. Outside that window, ask the admin to mark it for you.
- **Grading**: pick which result types apply to your course and enter marks. Marks stay a draft until you submit them for approval; students see them only after approval and publishing. You enter Sessional (quizzes, assignments) and Mid-term marks; Final and Practical marks are entered by the exam office. **Award list (PDF)** prints your subject's marks. In a program affiliated with a university (KMU, AUST …), the parts the university conducts (e.g. Mid and Final) cannot be added or entered: enter the internal marks only, and **Internal award list (PDF)** prints them with each student's Reg No for the university.
- **Substitute classes**: classes you take for a colleague on leave, as the administration confirmed them. Press **Mark…** to tick who is present (within the usual marking time).
- **Clinical supervision**: if the administration made you a clinical supervisor, this opens **My rotations**: mark each day's clinical attendance and sign your students' logbook entries (see the *Clinical supervisors* chapter).
- **Question bank**: add questions for the courses you teach: multiple choice (2 to 6 options and the right one), short or long (with an optional marking key), each with its marks, difficulty, Bloom level and CLO. **Change** your own questions until one is used in a final paper; **Retire** hides it from new papers. The exam office builds papers from the bank. Students never see it.
- **Course outcomes (OBE)**: for each of your classes, write its learning outcomes (CLOs), each with a Bloom level, and tick the program outcomes (PLOs) it serves (**Start from this course's last class** copies an earlier class's CLOs). Then say what each assessment measures: the whole assessment (one or more CLOs share its marks) or question parts (Q1, Q2 … each with its own marks and CLO, and **Part marks…** to enter them). **Send to the exam office**; once locked, the outcomes stay fixed. **Attainment** shows, per CLO, how many students reached it and whether the class attained it (the program's levels, normally 50% for a student and 60% of the students). Marks and results are not changed.
- **Self-assessments**: marks of practice quizzes, class tests and assignments done on paper in class. Press **New self-assessment** (title, kind, date, total marks, topic), then **Marks** to type each student's marks (tick Absent, add a remark) or upload a CSV with `roll_number,marks` (A = absent). Students and parents see their marks with the class average; you see the class at a glance (average, highest, lowest, under half). Self-assessments never count in exams or results.
- **Assignments**: set work, receive Google Drive links before the deadline, then accept or reject each one.
- **Students to watch**: students in your courses who may need help.
- **Student feedback**: what your students said about your courses. Anonymous, shown once a course's results are published.
- **Exam duties**: the papers you are on duty for, with the exam roll to mark, and the papers of the courses you teach.
- **Library**: your loans, the catalogue and research resources.

School mode only: **My class** (morning roll call and parent-teacher meetings), **Homework diary** and **Exam marks & report cards**.

## My Timetable

Your week at a glance; download it as a PDF.

**My available times** (a tab on My Timetable): tick the periods you cannot teach, and if you like, at most how many classes a day and periods in a row. When the administration makes the timetable with Smart timetable, you get no classes then. Your current timetable does not move. If the administration has fixed your times, you can see them but not change them.

**Free slots & extra classes** (a tab on My Timetable):

1. Pick your course. You see the periods when you, its students and a room are all free (days on approved leave and holidays are left out).
2. Choose **Make-up for a missed class** (a past class that was cancelled or had no attendance) or **Extra class** (course behind; give a reason).
3. Pick the room and press **Book**. The slot is held for you while an administrator decides. Once approved, the class appears on the timetable and attendance and your students are told.
4. **My requests** shows each request; **Withdraw** frees the slot.
