# Student

**Home** shows today's classes, notices, fees due and anything waiting for you. Sign in with your roll number.

## Study

- **My Courses**: enroll in your current semester's courses, or retake one you have taken before. Open a course for its topics, slides and notes.
- **My Timetable**: your week at a glance, with PDF download. Make-up and extra classes appear here once approved, and you get a notification.
- **My Attendance**: your attendance in every course. Below the minimum you are barred from the final exam.
- **Assignments**: work set by your teachers. Hand in a Google Drive link before the deadline.
- **My Exams**: published datesheets, whether you may sit each paper, and your admit card.
- **My Results**: marks and outcome for every subject once published, and your result card (transcript) with GPA and CGPA.
- **Course feedback**: tell the institute how each course went (anonymous).
- **Degree progress & documents**: how far you are through your degree and the documents you gave the office.

School mode only: **Diary & attendance** and **Report cards**.

## Services

- **My Fees**: every fee, its instalments and challans, discounts, payments and what is still due. Download challans to pay at the bank or pay online with 1Bill, and download receipts.
- **My Fines**: any fine against you and its status.
- **Library**: your loans and the catalogue; request a book.
- **Certificates**: ask the office for a certificate; download it once approved (and paid, if there is a fee).
- **Applications**: apply for leave or anything else and follow it.
- **Feedback & Complaints**: file a complaint and track it.
- **Calendar**: holidays, exams and deadlines.
- **Announcements**: official notices.

## Account

- **My Profile**: your record. Change your photo or contact details; the office approves each change.
- **My warnings**: formal warnings, if any.
