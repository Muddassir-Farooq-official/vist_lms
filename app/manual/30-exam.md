# Exam department

The exam department (Controller of Examinations) checks and publishes results, runs exams and re-sits, and prints result documents. **Home** shows results waiting for you, who to chase and the exams coming up. Administrators find the same tools under Academics.

## Grading tables and mark split

**Result approval → Grading tables & mark split** (administrators and result approvers):

- **Grading tables**: one table per affiliating body (KMU, AUST, Sarhad, Board ...). Each table holds the grades with their starting percentage and grade points (fixed, or sliding inside a band such as 76–79% = 3.6–3.9), the division and remark, the overall, theory and practical pass marks, the most a re-sit can earn, the GPA needed for promotion, probation and the degree, and whether results are by Semester (GPA) or Annual (total, % and division). Every value can be edited at any time; results already published never change.
- **Add ready-made drafts** adds starting tables marked **Draft**. Check every row against the official table, untick Draft, then give the table to programs. A draft cannot be used.
- **Programs: table and mark split**: pick each program's table and how a subject's marks are split into Sessional, Mid-term, Final and Practical. A subject can have its own split (for example a theory-only subject with Practical 0).
- **Which part each assessment counts in**: quizzes and assignments count in Sessional and are scaled to its marks.

Theory and practical must each be passed. Final and Practical marks are entered by the exam office; teachers enter Sessional and Mid-term.

## Degree-awarding or affiliated

Each program says who conducts its exams (**Grading tables & mark split → Programs → Exams conducted by**):

- **This institute** (a degree-awarding institute): the institute runs everything — sessional, mid, final and practical — and decides the result, as described below.
- **Affiliating university** (an affiliated college of KMU, AUST …): tick the parts the university conducts, e.g. *Mid* and *Final*. Then:
  1. Those parts cannot be added to a subject, their marks cannot be entered, and they cannot be put on the institute's datesheet.
  2. Teachers enter only the internal marks: Quiz 1, Quiz 2 … Quiz n, assignments, sessional, and any part the institute still runs (e.g. the mid when the university takes only the final). With a mark split, they are scaled to the internal marks (e.g. 5 quizzes of 10 = 50 → out of 20).
  3. **Internal award list (PDF / CSV)** in each subject (also on the teacher's Grading page) lists roll no., **Reg No**, every internal mark and the internal total, to send to the university. Students without a Reg No are shown in red and listed above the buttons, with the batch's deadline: fill them in on **University Reg Nos**.
  4. Approving and publishing the sheet shows students their internal marks; it decides no grade or pass/fail.
  5. When the university announces the result, open **University result**, choose the program and term, upload the CSV (Reg No or roll no., course code, the university's marks obtained and total and/or the grade, optional PASS/FAIL), check it and save. Students are told; transcripts, GPA/CGPA, result cards, the gazette and promotion use the university's result. Importing again replaces it when the university revises a result.
  6. **Supplementary (re-sit) results**: when the university announces a supplementary result, choose **Result of: a supplementary (re-sit) exam** and upload it the same way. It is accepted only for subjects the student failed in the university's result. It becomes the result that counts (GPA/CGPA, transcript marked *(S)*, promotion); the first attempt is kept on record and never lost. Importing the supplementary again revises it; a regular import can no longer overwrite a subject that has a supplementary result.

Practical example (KMU, BS Nursing, KMU takes Mid + Final): split Sessional 20 · Mid 30 · Final 50. The teacher enters Quiz 1 (15/20) and Quiz 2 (9/10) → internal 24/30 → **16 / 20** on the internal award list. KMU's result says 82/100 → the student gets KMU's grade for 82% on the KMU table and the subject counts in the GPA.

## Tutorial: setting up a BSN program with the AUST table

A worked example from start to finish. Change the names and numbers to your own.

### 1. Prepare the table

1. Open **Result approval → Grading tables & mark split** and press **Add ready-made drafts**. You now have AUST, KMU, Sarhad University and Board (annual), each marked **Draft**.
2. Press **Edit** on AUST. Compare every row with the university's official table and correct it. For AUST the draft has: pass 50% overall, theory 50%, practical 50%; a re-sit can earn at most 60%; GPA 2.00 to be promoted, probation from 1.50, CGPA 2.00 for the degree; sliding points (76–79% gives 3.6 to 3.9).
3. Untick **Draft** and press **Save table**. Do the same for KMU, Sarhad and the Board with their official tables. KMU and Sarhad drafts are partly placeholders: replace them completely.

### 2. Give programs their table and split

1. In **Programs: table and mark split**, choose AUST for BSN.
2. Type the split for BSN, for example Sessional 20, Mid 30, Final 50, Practical 50, and press **Save split**. Every BSN subject now has 100 theory marks and 50 practical marks.
3. A theory-only subject (for example English) needs its own split: **+ Subject split**, choose English, set Practical to 0 and press **Save**.

### 3. Tag the catalogue

In **Which part each assessment counts in** set Mid Term to Mid-term, Final to Final, Practical / Viva to Practical / clinical. Leave quizzes and assignments as Sessional: they are added together and scaled to the Sessional marks.

### 4. A worked result

Ayesha's marks in Anatomy: quizzes 16 of 20, mid 21 of 30, final 60 of 100, practical 20 of 50.

- Sessional 16 of 20 = **16**; Mid 21 of 30 = **21**; Final 60 of 100 scaled to 50 = **30**. Theory = 16 + 21 + 30 = **67 of 100 = 67%**: passed (50% needed).
- Practical 20 of 50 = **40%**: failed (50% needed).
- Overall (67 + 20) of 150 = **58%**, but because the practical was failed the subject is **Fail (Practical)**.

### 5. Publish, re-sit, promote

1. The teacher enters quiz and mid marks; you (the exam office) enter final and practical marks. Approve and publish as usual. Print the **Award list (PDF)**.
2. **Supplementary exams**: register Ayesha. She re-sits only the practical paper. She scores 40 of 50, so the practical is passed; her overall becomes (67 + 40) of 150 = 71.3%, but AUST caps a re-sit at 60%, so she gets **60%** and her grade from the 60% band. The result card shows **(S)**.
3. **Class results & promotion**: choose BSN, semester 2, the term, and press **Show**. Everyone's GPA and status appears: GPA 2.00 or more is Promoted, 1.50 to 1.99 Promoted on probation, below 1.50 Not promoted. Print the **Result gazette** and **Result cards**, tick who moves up and press **Promote**.

### 6. Annual (board) programs

Use the Board (annual) table. "Semester" then means the year (semester 1 = 1st year). Results show total marks, % and division instead of GPA, and promotion needs every subject passed.

## Result approval

- Teachers submit each subject's marks. In **Waiting for approval** open a subject, check the marks (the Result column is a projection until published), then **Approve** or **Send back…** with a reason. **Ready to publish** → **Publish to students**. Students see nothing until you publish.
- **Award list (PDF)** in each subject prints every student's marks, theory and practical %, grade and result, with the affiliating body and signature lines. For an affiliated program it is the **Internal award list (PDF / CSV)** instead.
- **University result**: import an affiliated program's result from the university (see *Degree-awarding or affiliated* above).
- **Result cards**: find a student to see or download their full result card (transcript).
- **Default scale**: the grading scale for programs without a table of their own.

A student below the attendance minimum is barred from the final exam and the subject shows **Short attendance**; it must be repeated.

## Class results & promotion

Pick a program, semester (year, for annual programs) and term, then **Show**:

- Everyone's GPA (or total, % and division), status (Promoted, Promoted on probation, Not promoted, Result pending, No subjects this term), subjects still to clear and the position holders.
- **Result cards (PDF)**: one page per student. **Result gazette (PDF)**: the result notification in roll order with position holders and signature lines.
- Tick who moves up and press **Promote**. **Undo this promotion** moves them back if needed.

## Other menu items

- **Result types**: the catalogue of assessments with their marks and their part of the split.
- **Datesheets & admit cards**: plan each exam's papers, put teachers on duty, fix clashes, publish and print admit cards. Students see a datesheet only after it is published.
- **Supplementary exams**: students who failed a subject on marks re-sit only the failed part: the final paper for theory, the practical paper for practical, two papers when both were failed. Register them, enter the marks (or mark absent), then publish. The table's re-sit cap limits the percentage; a pass shows as (S).
- **Student lookup**: find a student and their transcript. Read-only.
- **University Reg Nos**: fill in or correct each student's university registration number, one by one or from a CSV (download the class list, fill the `reg_number` column, upload, check the changes, save). It is printed on award lists, admit cards, result cards and transcripts. Set each batch's **Deadlines** there: after the last day an admit card is held while the Reg No is missing, unless you **Exempt…** that student with a reason.
- **Reports**: academic reports to print or download.
