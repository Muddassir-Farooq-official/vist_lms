# Principal and head of department

## Principal

- **Institute overview**: how the institute is doing right now. Read-only.
- **Teacher progress**: each teacher's position in their course against where they should be.
- **Course feedback**: students' anonymous feedback per course and teacher.
- **Student lookup**: find any student and see their record and transcript.
- **Monthly report**: one month at a glance, also sent on the 1st.
- **Warnings**: formal warnings; decide the action on a third warning.
- **Reports**: print or download reports.
- **Staff attendance**: arrivals, departures, late and early marks.
- **Hostel** and **Fixed assets**: read the hostel and asset records.
- **Activity log**: everything done in the system.
- **Calendar** and **Post an announcement**.

## Head of department (HOD)

- **Attendance oversight**: classes not marked and attendance across your department.
- **Teacher progress**, **Course feedback**, **Student lookup**, **Reports**, **Calendar** and **Post an announcement**, as above.
