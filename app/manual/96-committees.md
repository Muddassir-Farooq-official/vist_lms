# Committees

Committees (academic council, discipline, quality and so on) keep their meetings, minutes and decisions here. Only a committee's members and the administration see its minutes; the person an action is given to also sees that action.

## Committees

**Committees** lists the committees you are on, with their members and the next meeting. The administration makes committees: a name, a purpose, and the members, one per line, with their employee code and role, for example `T-001 chair`, `T-014 secretary`, `A-003 member`. Every committee has one chair. Members are staff; a student or parent cannot be added. **Change** shows the current members to edit.

## Meetings and minutes

Open a committee's **Meetings**. The chair or secretary schedules a meeting (date, time, place and agenda); every member is told. After the meeting they write who was present, the minutes and the decisions, and **Save minutes**. The chair (or the administration) presses **Make final** once the meeting has happened: final minutes can no longer change. **Minutes (PDF)** prints them. A meeting with no actions can be cancelled.

## Actions

The chair or secretary gives actions: what must be done, who does it (employee code) and by when. The owner is told, sees it under **My actions**, and is reminded once a day from 9 AM when it is due tomorrow, due today or overdue, until it is marked **Done**. The chair or secretary can also mark an action done or drop it. The administration sees **Every open action**.
