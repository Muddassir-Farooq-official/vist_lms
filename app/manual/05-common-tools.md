# Tools every staff member has

These items appear in the menu of most staff portals. They work the same everywhere.

## Announcements

Official notices from the institute, with their attachments. New ones also arrive as notifications.

## Post an announcement

Write a notice for students or colleagues. It goes live once the admin approves it, issued in your name.

## Applications

Apply to the administration for leave, a certificate or anything else, and follow it to a decision. You are notified when it is approved, rejected or when the office asks a question.

## Tasks

Give work to colleagues and receive it. Every task, answer and deadline is recorded. Accept or turn down a task with a reason, and mark it done when finished.

## Purchase requests

Ask for something to be bought, and follow it from approval to delivery.

## My pay & attendance

Your monthly salary slips, with every allowance and deduction, and your own attendance record. Download any payslip as a PDF.

## Calendar

Holidays, exams, events, term dates and deadlines. A holiday cancels that day's classes and excuses staff attendance.

## My Profile

Your record as held by the institute. Click your photo to change it, and update your contact details. The office approves every change before it shows.

## My warnings

Formal warnings from the administration. Each one counts for 12 months; a 3rd one within 12 months is final.

## Feedback & Complaints

File a complaint against a student or staff member, and track your own.
