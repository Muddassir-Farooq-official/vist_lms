# Parent

Parents see every child linked to their account.

- **My children**: attendance, exams, results and fees for each child.
- **Fees**: every fee, its challans to download and pay, and receipts.
- **Marks**: quiz, assignment and exam marks as teachers enter them. The final grade comes when results are published.
- **Timetable & exams**: classes for the next two weeks and the exam datesheet.
- **Notices**: announcements for your child's class.
- **Calendar**: holidays, exams and events.
- **My account**: your email for notifications and your password.

School mode only: **Diary & attendance**, **Parent-teacher meetings** (book a time with the class teacher) and **Report cards**.
