# Parent

Parents see every child linked to their account.

- **My children**: attendance, exams, results and fees for each child.
- **Your children today**: at the top of the home page, one card per child: today's classes and attendance, what is to pay with **Pay now** (the challan, and the number for 1Bill or your bank app), the next exam and the latest result, and the transport route, stop and pick-up time if your child uses the institute's transport. You are also told if your child is absent at the hostel night roll call. Allow notifications on your phone to be told when a child is marked absent in a class or a result is published.
- **Fees**: every fee, its challans to download and pay, and receipts.
- **Marks**: quiz, assignment and exam marks as teachers enter them. The final grade comes when results are published.
- **Self-assessments**: your child's marks in practice quizzes and class tests, with the class average. They never count in results.
- **Timetable & exams**: classes for the next two weeks and the exam datesheet.
- **Notices**: announcements for your child's class.
- **Calendar**: holidays, exams and events.
- **My account**: your email for notifications and your password.

School mode only: **Diary & attendance**, **Parent-teacher meetings** (book a time with the class teacher) and **Report cards**.
