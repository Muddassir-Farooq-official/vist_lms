# Accounts office

The accounts office records fees and payments, follows up dues and keeps the books. **Home** shows today's collection, this month's money and what is waiting for you.

## Fees

**Fees** has these tabs:

- **Fee types**: tuition, admission, hostel, ID card and so on.
- **Fee structures**: a configured charge: which fee type, which term, how much, how it is split into instalments, the late fee and which programs and semesters it applies to. Activating one charges every matching student.
  - **Term**: pick the batch's own term for a fee meant for one batch. For a fee covering several batches choose **Each student's own term**: every student then sees the term of their own batch. **Term…** corrects the term on an existing fee.
  - **Change due date…**: move the due date for everyone on the fee, or only one program, semester or section. A live preview shows how many students move and any late fines already built up. Administrators only.
- **Assignments**: each student's charges. Open one to record a payment, see the statement and print receipts and challans.
  - **Change one student's due date**: the accounts office can do this; if it would remove a late fine already built up you are asked first whether to keep or remove it (with a reason).
  - **Move to another fee…**: a payment recorded on the wrong fee (for example tuition money entered on Hostel) is moved to the right fee of the same student after an administrator approves. The right fee counts as paid on the original day; the receipt keeps its number and names the right fee; the wrong fee gets a 7-day fine-free pause.
- **Student accounts**: find a student (also by father's name or CNIC) and see every fee, payment and what is due.
- **Scholarships & discounts** and **Previous dues** (dues carried forward from before the system).

### Late fines

A fee can carry a late fee per day. When recording a payment or changing a date would make a late fine disappear, the system asks whether to keep or remove it and records the reason.

## Other menu items

- **Student lookup**: find a student and open their record and fee account. Read-only.
- **Accounts**: money in and money out: expenses, other income, vendors.
- **Reports** and **Finance reports**: who owes what and for how long, daily collections by collector, vendor balances, bank statement matching and exports for the accountant.
- **Refunds**: request a refund; an administrator (not you) approves it; then pay it out and print the voucher. Nothing can be refunded beyond what was paid.
- **1Bill payments**: fees paid online through 1Bill (bank apps, JazzCash, Easypaisa, ATMs). Match each day's total with the bank's settlement.
- **Inventory / Store** and **Fixed assets**: stock and long-lasting property.
- **My approval requests**: requests you sent that need an administrator (fee waivers, payment moves, write-offs), with their status.
- **Payroll**: monthly pay for staff.
- **Fines**: fines against students and employees from any source.
