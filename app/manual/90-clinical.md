# Clinical supervisors

This chapter is for hospital staff and teachers who supervise students' clinical training.

## Signing in

The administration gives a hospital supervisor a username and a temporary password. At the first sign-in you choose your own password. Your login opens only **My rotations**: the clinical groups given to you.

## My rotations

Each row is a group at a ward for a date range, with its days and hours, and how many logbook entries are waiting for your signature. Press **Open**.

- **Attendance**: choose the day (today by default) and mark each student present, absent or on leave, then **Save attendance**. Only the rotation's days can be marked, never a day that has not come. You can correct up to a week back; older days are corrected by the administration.
- **Logbook**: students add what they did (the skill, the day, how many times). **Sign** it, or press **Not signed…** and say why: the student sees your note and can add a corrected entry. A signed or rejected entry stays on record.
- **Interns**: interns placed with you appear below your rotations. **Verify** each duty they log (day, shift, hours), or **Reject…** with a reason they see. Evaluate each intern (1 to 5 on clinical skills, professionalism, communication, punctuality and patient care, with a comment) and **Save evaluation**; you can evaluate more than once during a placement.
