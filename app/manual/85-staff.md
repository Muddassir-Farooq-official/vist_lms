# General staff

**Home** shows your day, your tasks and your requests in one place.

- **Tasks**: work given to you; accept, turn down with a reason, or mark done.
- **My pay & attendance**: payslips and your attendance.
- **Announcements** and **Post an announcement**.
- **Applications**, **Purchase requests** and **Feedback & Complaints**: your requests.
- **Calendar**, **My Profile** and **My warnings**.

Each is explained in "Tools every staff member has".
