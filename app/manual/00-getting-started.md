# Getting started

VIST LMS is one portal for the whole institute. Administrators, the accounts office, the exam department, teachers, students, parents, the library, the hostel, the gate and general staff each sign in to their own part of it and see only what their job needs.

## Signing in

1. Open the institute's portal address in any browser (phone or computer).
2. Type your username and password. Students use their roll number; staff use the username the office gave them.
3. The first time, the system asks you to choose a new password. Keep it to yourself.

Forgot your password? Press **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and follow the steps, or ask the office to reset it.

## Finding your way

- The **side menu** lists everything you can do. On a phone, the most used items sit in the bar at the bottom and **More** opens the full menu.

### Finding your way in the menu

- The menu is grouped by job. Tap a group's name to open or close it. The group of the page you are on is always open; the others stay the way you left them.
- **Search the menu**: the box at the top of the menu. Type part of a page's name or another word for it (for example *challan*, *salary* or *KMU*) and press Enter to open the first match. On a computer, press **/** or **Ctrl+K** anywhere to jump to it.
- A number beside a page is work waiting for you there: approvals, profile changes, certificate requests, new admission forms, result sheets to approve, applications, tasks and unread announcements. A closed group shows the total of its numbers.
- The **bell** at the top shows notifications: results published, fees due, approvals waiting and more.
- The **version** box in the menu shows which version you are using; **What's new** lists what changed in each version.
- **Help & manual** (in the menu) opens this manual at your own chapter. You can download it as a PDF.

## Good habits

- Sign out on shared computers.
- Most actions are recorded in the activity log with who did them and when. Money and results can never be quietly edited: corrections are made by adding a new, recorded entry.
- When a screen asks for a reason, write a real one. It is kept with the record.

## Schools

When the institute is set up as a school (Settings → Institute type), the menus use school words: **Subjects** for courses, **Classes** for programs and semesters, and **Classes & sections** replaces Programs. A school takes one morning roll call (**My class**, **Diary & attendance**) and uses **Exam marks & report cards** instead of course grading and GPA results. Everything else in this manual works the same.
